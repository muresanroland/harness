ORQADENCE — 12×12 TERMINAL LOGO

Grid: two 5×5 panes per row, 2-column and 2-row gaps → exactly 12 columns × 12 rows.
Unlit pane = rounded outline drawn in the pane's own color.
Lit pane   = 5×5 cells: the inner cells filled with the pane's own color as BACKGROUND (█ below
             marks those cells; render them as spaces with a bg color), with a bold ❯ in #000a1a on the
             centre cell; its edge cells are half and quarter blocks (▗▄▖ ▐▌ ▝▀▘) in the pane's color, so
             the fill ends mid-cell where an unlit pane's outline runs and both panes are one size.
Note: terminal cells are ~2× taller than wide, so 12×12 cells looks tall; the header uses the 13×6 mark instead.
